NEON HANDS

A holographic workstation: your webcam becomes a hand scanner, and your fingers become the mouse. Pinch to grab the object and drag it through space — open your palm to scale it, make a fist to squash it, point to spin it, throw a ✌️ to morph its geometry, 👍 for a confirmation flash.

No camera? Demo Mode drives the full gesture engine with a simulated hand so you can see every feature. Mouse also works: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
