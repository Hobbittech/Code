Adventure Game Answer Sheet
==========================

How to Play & Navigation:
-------------------------
- Move using: north, east, south, west, up, down, in, out
  Example: To go north, type 'north'.
- Use 'look' in each room to see exits, items, NPCs, and enemies.
- Use 'map' to see your current room and all available directions.
- Use 'inventory' to check your items.
- Use 'health' to check your health.
- Use 'talk [npc]' to interact with characters.
- Use 'use [item]' in the right place (rope at cliff, torch in cave, etc).
- Use 'quit' to exit the game.

Walkthrough & Quests (Step-by-Step):
------------------------------------
1. Get the key in the library (north_room).
2. Go east to east_room, use the key to open the chest, take the sword.
3. Go east to garden, take the gold coin.
4. Go north to final_gate. You need a shield to pass.
5. Go back to library, then east to armory, take the shield.
6. Go west to west_room, defeat the goblin (use sword).
7. Go north to treasure_room, take the rope.
8. Go west to cliff_edge, use rope to climb down to cave_entrance.
9. Take the torch in cave_entrance, use it in deep_cave to see.
10. Go in to deep_cave, defeat the giant spider, take the ancient amulet.
11. Go up to cliff_edge, then back to tower (north from library), take potion.
12. Go up to wizard_lab, talk to wizard, take magic ring.
13. Go back to garden, east to merchant_stall, trade gold coin for potion.
14. Go north to final_gate, use shield to pass, climb mountain_path.
15. Go up to dragon_lair, defeat dragon (must have magic ring), take dragon egg.
16. Explore forest: north from garden to forest_edge, take herbs.
17. North to deep_forest, defeat wild boar, east to river_bank, take fishing rod.
18. East to old_bridge, east to ruins, take silver key.
19. North to haunted_house, defeat ghost, up to attic, take diary.
20. Down to secret_tunnel, north to underground_lake, defeat giant eel, take pearl.
21. South to village_square, take bread, talk to baker.
22. East to inn, rest to restore health, talk to innkeeper.
23. West to blacksmith, bring ore from mountain_pass/mines to upgrade sword.
24. South to farm, east to meadow, defeat wolf, take wool, return to farmer for reward.
25. East to forest_clearing, meet fairy, take healing flower.
26. North to enchanted_forest, defeat unicorn, take magic scroll.
27. North to mountain_pass, take rare ore, north to mines, take gem and ore.
28. East to deep_mines, defeat bat and goblin king, take treasure chest.
29. North to castle_hall (must have gem), talk to king, accept quest to defeat dragon and bring dragon egg.
30. Complete king's quest for ultimate victory.

Tips & Secrets:
---------------
- Use 'look' in every room to find items, NPCs, and exits.
- Use 'map' to see your current location and directions.
- Upgrade your sword at the blacksmith for easier battles.
- Rest at the inn to restore health if needed.
- Help villagers for extra rewards and items.
- Collect gems, scrolls, and rare items for castle entry and king's quest.
- Explore all areas for secrets, treasures, and side quests.

Victory:
--------
- Survive all enemies, complete the king's quest, collect treasures, and explore every area.
- Find all secrets and side quests for the best ending!
