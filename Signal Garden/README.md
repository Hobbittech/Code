# Signal Garden

A small, self-contained browser puzzle. Rotate the path tiles to carry a signal from the spark to the flower, then take a slow walk through the next part of the garden.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No install, server, account, or build step is needed. Your unlocked level and completed blooms are saved in the browser on your device.

- Click or tap a path tile to turn it clockwise.
- Use **Enter** or **Space** to turn a focused tile. Use the arrow keys to move between path tiles.
- **Show me one tile** gently points to a tile that needs turning.
- Use the sound button if you would like quiet, optional turn sounds.

The game is designed for mouse, touch, keyboard, narrow screens, and reduced-motion preferences.
