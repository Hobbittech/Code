Innovation Project

Our research project is all about finding answers to our endless questions about looting and illegal antiquities trade. We were really excited to investigate a perfect solution to this gigantic problem archaeologist have to face. We researched a lot about the topic and found out that looting is a huge problem that affects many countries around the world. It not only destroys archaeological sites but also leads to the loss of valuable cultural heritage. We wanted to find a way to help prevent looting and protect these sites.
