Our Robot

Our Robot offers a range of versatility and adaptability while being compact and not too heavy. Our wise use of motors and our collection of clip-on attachments (that our skilled builders have conceived) make for a more efficient playthrough. Lastly, we have a sleek profile and a low center of gravity, which boosts stability when operating.

Strategy

Our strategy is to try to complete as many challenges at once and as fast as possible to save time.
